# Risk Register

For projects with management extensions enabled, `RISKS.json` is authoritative
and `RISKS.md` is its generated view. Use `risk_manager.py` to create, review,
mitigate or accept risks. Keep this legacy document only for historical context;
do not maintain a competing set of statuses in its table.
